Welcome to District Central Karachi

Dear Fellow Karachiites,

It is my pleasure to welcome you to the official District Central digital platform, designed to provide easy access to public services, government information, and an efficient grievance redressal system. This initiative reflects our commitment to transparent, accountable, and citizen-centric governance by bringing essential services together in one convenient portal.

Through this platform, citizens can file complaints, access government schemes and programs, connect with relevant departments, and explore public services. I encourage all residents of District Central to use this platform and share their valuable feedback as we continue to improve our services.

Deputy Commissioner

Taha Saleem

It is an honor to serve as the Deputy Commissioner of District Central, Karachi, a district that stands at the heart of the city's civic and economic life. My commitment remains steadfast: to ensure transparent governance, efficient public service delivery, and swift redressal of citizen grievances for every resident of District Central.

Under the guidance of the Commissioner Karachi and the Government of Sindh, we are working to strengthen revenue administration, streamline the issuance of Domicile and Permanent Resident Certificates, improve market price monitoring, and expand digital facilitation across all branches of this office. These efforts are aimed at making government services more accessible, accountable, and responsive to the needs of our citizens.

I invite the residents of District Central to make full use of this platform, to raise their concerns, and to share their valuable suggestions. Together, we can build a district administration that citizens trust and rely on.

Frequently Asked Questions

Citizens may submit a domicile application with required identity documents, proof of residence, photographs, and supporting records at the designated facilitation counter.

Complaints can be submitted through the online complaint form, the district complaint cell, or official contact channels with complete details for review and follow-up.

The Deputy Commissioner Office location details will be published in the contact section with office address, public dealing hours, and visitor guidance.

Official forms, notifications, circulars, and public documents can be accessed from the downloads section after selecting the required service category.

Citizens may review the Assistant Commissioners section to identify the relevant sub-division and use the listed official contact channel for assistance.

The latest official price list will be available in the citizen services and downloads areas for public reference and market awareness.
